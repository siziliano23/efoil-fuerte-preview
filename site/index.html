How it works

Three parts. Then a ride back.

15min

Briefing on the sand

Wetsuit, helmet and vest on. You learn the board, the hand remote and the safety rules before you get wet.

60min

On the water

Kneeling first, then standing, then lifting off. Pace depends on you and the conditions, not on a schedule.

15min

Feedback

What worked, what to change, and what your next session would look like if you want one.

Pick-up is separate from the 90 minutes. Siam collects you from hotels between Las Playitas and Morro Jable and drops you back afterwards. Tell him where you're staying when you book.

Where

The beach depends on the day.

Efoil Fuerte is based in La Pared on the west coast, but sessions run on the calmer east coast between Tarajalejo and Jandía. Siam picks the spot with the best water for the day and tells you the beach the evening before.

Wind, swell and where you're staying all go into that call. That's the point of a small operation: it can move.

  • La Pared, Calle Picon 1BBase and workshop. Not usually the session beach.
  • Tarajalejo · La LajitaSession beaches, north end
  • Costa CalmaSession beaches, middle
  • Jandía · Morro JableSession beaches, south end
Hotel pick-up covers the east coast from Las Playitas to Morro Jable. Staying elsewhere? Ask, and you'll get a meeting point.

If he cancels for wind, sea state or safety, you get a full refund or a new date. Forecasts alone don't decide it; he looks at the water on the day.
