Your coach

Questions

Useful answers before you message.

How long is a standard session?

Each standard session lasts 1.5 hours: 15 minutes of briefing, 60 minutes on the water and 15 minutes of feedback.

What equipment is included?

The board, wetsuit, helmet and impact vest are included. Hotel transfer is also listed as included.

Where does the session take place?

Efoil Fuerte is based at Calle Picon 1B, La Pared, Pájara. Confirm the exact meeting or pickup point before travelling.

Are there age, weight or swimming requirements?

Those limits are not currently published clearly. Send Siam the rider’s age, approximate weight and swimming confidence so suitability can be confirmed before booking.

What happens if conditions are unsuitable?

Sessions depend on safe conditions. Ask for the written weather, rescheduling and cancellation rules before making payment.

Are photos or videos included?

They are not listed as part of the audited standard inclusions. Confirm availability and price with Siam rather than assuming they are included.

Can I buy a gift voucher?

Gift vouchers are part of the published offer. Ask Siam which session and delivery options are currently available.

Hotel or activity partner?

For guest referrals, pickup coordination or private group arrangements, start a separate partner conversation instead of using the customer booking flow.
